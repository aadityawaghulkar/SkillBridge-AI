How SkillBridge AI Predicts Your Path

SkillBridge AI converts your selected skills into a high-dimensional feature vector and calculates Euclidean cosine distances against weighted industry benchmarks.

01
Vectorization
Your 20 skill signals are mapped into a binary feature vector V = [x₁, x₂, ..., x₂₀].
02
k-NN Cluster Matching
The engine calculates nearest neighbor distances to 12 career centroids in target feature space.
03
Weighted Skill Analysis
Skill deficits are categorized as must-have (3x weight), good-to-have (1.5x), or optional (0.5x).
04
Roadmap Generation
Missing skills are sequenced into a multi-week timeline ordered by dependency and priority.
KNN Model Hyperparameter Playground

Adjusting K controls the number of top career cluster rankings calculated in real time.
